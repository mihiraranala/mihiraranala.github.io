Save Aarey was a public initiative opposing the felling of trees in Mumbai's Aarey Colony for the proposed Mumbai Metro car shed. As part of the campaign, Mihir used the U.S. EPA's Greenhouse Gas Equivalencies Calculator to run carbon sequestration equivalency calculations on the trees slated for removal, translating their carbon capture value into everyday, relatable terms to build a data-driven argument for preserving the urban forest instead of clearing it for infrastructure.
