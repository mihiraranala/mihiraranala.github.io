Vilnius Connect is an urban design competition entry that reimagines the railway precinct in Vilnius, Lithuania as a catalyst for urban transformation, positioning the station as the beating heart of the city. The proposal studies a 1km radius around the Vilnius railway station, addressing transit inefficiencies and heritage preservation while promoting sustainable, human-centered urban design. Key interventions include a reorganized Gelezinkelio street anchored by an e-mobility hub that prioritizes cycling and pedestrian infrastructure, a network of above-ground and underground corridors (including a cyclist tunnel) connecting residential neighborhoods and heritage districts, and expanded green-blue infrastructure through new plazas, a Heritage Park, and green roofs.
